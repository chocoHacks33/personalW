I love to live in the moment. I love the thrill. I'm Prakash, 21, an avid basketball player, and someone who loves the game for the intensity and mindset it builds both on and off the court. To me, it's as much a mental game as it is physical: reading people, making split-second decisions, and staying locked in.

As a kid, I have always been interested in building things at the intersection of technology and real-world impact. What started as experimenting with code and systems has grown into designing and shipping full-stack products that solve real problems.

Currently, I'm pursuing Computer Science at the National University of Singapore, where I focus on software engineering, systems, and product development. Along the way, I've built full-stack applications, data pipelines, and backend systems from analytics dashboards.

Beyond engineering, I'm deeply interested in startups and growth. We built a student founder community across universities. Through The Collective, I've intentionally placed myself around early-stage founders, learning firsthand how people build from zero, understanding their challenges, and using that exposure to shape how I approach building my own ventures.

On the product side, I'm building admorph.ai, where we rethink how advertising systems are built. Instead of static creatives, we generate and distribute thousands of personalized ad variants. Essentially, we built a scalable system that matches content to the right audience segments and optimizes engagement.

I'm also borderline obsessed with hackathons. I seriously love to actively "farm" them as a way to stress-test ideas, build fast under pressure, and continuously improve how I execute. These experiences have shaped how I think about speed, iteration, and building things that actually work.
